What Are Screen Readers?
Screen readers are software applications that enable visually impaired users to read the text displayed on a computer screen with a speech synthesizer or braille display. They interpret the content and structure of web pages to provide accessible navigation.
Why Semantic HTML Matters
Divs vs. Real Elements
Using only <div> tags makes it harder for screen readers to understand your site’s structure. Semantic tags like <nav>, <header>, <section>, <a> and <footer> provide meaning, which helps assistive technologies guide users more effectively.

Tips for Accessibility
Use semantic HTML whenever possible. Always provide alt text for images, ensure proper heading hierarchy, and test your website with at least one screen reader to ensure compatibility.
